HOLY PEAK - a pilgrimage up the silent mountain
================================================

Starting the game
-----------------
Windows : open the HolyPeak folder and run HolyPeak.exe.
          If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click
          "More info" and then "Run anyway" (the game is not code-signed).
macOS   : drag "Holy Peak.app" anywhere (e.g. Applications) and open it.
          The first time, right-click the app and choose Open, then Open
          again (the game is not notarized by Apple). If macOS still refuses,
          run this once in Terminal:
              xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Holy Peak.app"
Linux   : extract the archive and run ./HolyPeak inside the HolyPeak folder.

No installation or .NET runtime is needed. On the very first launch the
soundtrack is generated in the background, which takes a few seconds; after
that it is cached.

Controls
--------
Every action can be rebound (two keys each, plus a gamepad button) under
Options > Controls. These are the defaults:

                    Keyboard         Alternate       Gamepad
Move / look         Arrow keys       WASD            Left stick / D-pad
Jump / open kite    Z                Space           A
Strike              X                J               X
Ring the bell       C                K               Y
Cast a psalm        F                I               RB or RT
Heal (hold)         V                L               B, LB or LT
Rest / talk / use   Up               W               Up
Map                 Tab              M               Back
Pause               Esc              P               Start
Fullscreen          F11

Moving
------
Press toward a ledge you can reach to pull yourself up onto it.
Hold Down and strike while running to vault forward on your staff.
Jump at a rope, vine or chain to catch it: push left and right to swing,
Up and Down to climb, and jump to let go with the speed of the swing.
Strike a hanging lantern to be thrown away from it, or from above to bounce.
Hold Down and strike in the air to bounce off creatures and upward thorns.
Hold Down and jump on a thin ledge to drop through it.

Grace, psalms and charms
------------------------
Every blow your staff lands gathers grace (the bar of light under your
flames). Hold Heal to turn grace into a flame of vigil, or press Cast to
spend the same grace on a psalm: it hits for half as much as your staff, but
at range or over an area. Hold Down or Up while casting for other psalms
once you have found them.

Charms change how you play. Wear them when you rest at a shrine, as many as
your notches allow.

Rest at shrines to heal, change charms and save. If you fall, you wake at the
last shrine and your embers wait where you fell: go back for them.

Assist options (game speed, invincibility) are in Options.
Saves are written to a "HolyPeak" folder in your user application-data folder.
Saves from Holy Peak 2.0 carry over.
